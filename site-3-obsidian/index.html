The premise

A kitchen is not a floor plan. It is a room that behaves.

At 7am it should be quiet, bright and out of your way. At 9pm it should be low, warm and the best place in the house to stand with a glass of wine. Most kitchens can only manage one of those. We design for both, and for the eleven hours in between.

01

Light as a material

Four independent lighting states, set on scenes. The room you cook in and the room you entertain in are the same room wearing different clothes.

02

Nothing you don't touch

No handles you don't need, no appliance faces you didn't ask for. Everything that isn't used every day disappears behind joinery.

03

Stone chosen in person

We bring slabs to your house, not photographs to a showroom. Veining is a composition decision and it should be made in your own light.

For projects above $250,000 we will take you to Carrara, or bring three full slabs to your driveway — whichever you would rather. Either way the stone is chosen as a whole composition, and the veining is laid out on paper before a single cut is made.

The method

Five chapters.

One principal from the first conversation to the last. You will never be handed to a project manager you have not met.

01

The session

Two hours at your home, at dusk, because that is when a kitchen's lighting problems reveal themselves. We leave with measurements and you leave with a written investment band.

02

The film

Rather than a floor plan, we produce a short walkthrough of your kitchen as it will be — lighting states, materials, sightlines. Most clients watch it a dozen times before approving.

03

The materials

Slabs and finishes come to your house. Nothing is decided under showroom lighting, and nothing is decided in one sitting.

04

The build

Cabinetry from our Italian and local partners, installed by one crew. Condo boards, elevator windows and COIs are handled by us, not by you.

05

The reveal

We stage, light and photograph the room, then hand you the lighting scenes on your phone. Punch list closed within ten days.

Questions

Answered without theatre.

Real numbers for the Miami market, including the parts other studios avoid.

How much does a luxury kitchen remodel cost in Miami?

An Obsidian kitchen runs $150,000 to $450,000. The band is set by how much structure moves, whether cabinetry is European-imported or built locally, and the stone. Waterfront and high-rise projects sit at the upper end because of access, HOA requirements and hurricane-rated glazing.

How long does a high-end kitchen remodel take in Miami?

Six to nine months. Design and material curation take eight to twelve weeks, imported cabinetry runs fourteen to twenty weeks from Italy, installation is eight to twelve weeks. Condo and HOA approval in Miami-Dade typically adds four to six weeks, which we run in parallel with design rather than after it.

Do you work in high-rise condominiums?

Yes — roughly half our work is in Brickell, Bal Harbour and Key Biscayne towers. We handle the building application, the certificate of insurance, elevator scheduling and the noise-window restrictions that most contractors discover in week two.

What makes Obsidian different from other Miami kitchen companies?

We design the room as an experience rather than a floor plan — lighting states, materials, sightlines, and how the space behaves at 7am and again at 9pm. We take twelve projects a year, every one led by a principal, and we run no showroom: slabs and samples come to your home instead.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — fixed-rate terms from 60 to 180 months through private lending partners, plus construction-draw arrangements above $250,000. Pre-qualification uses a soft credit pull and does not affect your credit score. See investment & financing →

Which areas does Obsidian serve?

Coral Gables, Pinecrest, Coconut Grove, Key Biscayne, Bal Harbour, Miami Beach, Brickell and Sunny Isles. Because the studio takes twelve projects a year, the calendar is set by quarter rather than by waiting list. See the studio →
