Twelve a year. Five of them.

A Mediterranean revival villa, a Brickell tower, a beach house on Key Biscayne. Different problems entirely — the same two hours at dusk started each one.

Begin yours

Coral Gables · 7 months

Villa Alcázar.

A 1994 kitchen inside a 1926 house, pretending to be neither. We kept the arches and the barrel ceiling, removed everything else, and let a single book-matched slab carry the room.

Structural opening to the loggia · Italian lacquer · Book-matched quartzite · Four lighting scenes

Brickell · 9 months

Tower 41.

Forty-first floor, a two-hour service elevator window, and a board that had rejected the previous contractor twice. The kitchen faces west, so the entire lighting scheme was designed around a sunset that arrives through glass.

High-rise commission · HOA and elevator coordination by the studio · Marine-grade substrates throughout
