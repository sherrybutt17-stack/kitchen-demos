Service areas

Where does Obsidian work?

Across Coral Gables, Pinecrest, Coconut Grove, Key Biscayne, Bal Harbour, Miami Beach, Brickell and Sunny Isles, Florida. Because the studio takes twelve commissions a year, the calendar is set by quarter rather than by waiting list.

We already hold approved contractor status in a number of Brickell and Bal Harbour buildings, which removes several weeks from the front of those projects.

01

Coral Gables

Mediterranean revival houses with kitchens added in the eighties and nineties. The work is usually about undoing a previous renovation rather than the original house.

02

Pinecrest & Coconut Grove

Large lots, generous ceilings, and the space to let a kitchen continue into a living volume. Our most architecturally ambitious work happens here.

03

Brickell & Miami Beach

High-rise commissions. Board applications, COIs, elevator windows and noise restrictions handled by the studio — we hold approved status in several towers already.

04

Key Biscayne & Bal Harbour

Salt air and full-height glazing. Every specification here is marine-grade, and the lighting is designed around water reflecting onto the ceiling.

05

Sunny Isles & beyond

We occasionally take work in Fort Lauderdale and Palm Beach when the project suits the studio. Ask — the answer is sometimes yes.
