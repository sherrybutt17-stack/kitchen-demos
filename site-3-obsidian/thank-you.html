What happens next

Three steps to the film.

01

Within one business day

A principal emails you two evening slots. Reply with whichever suits — there is no scheduling link and no assistant in between.

02

At dusk, at your house

Two hours. We measure, photograph, test the existing lighting and talk through how the room fails you. You receive the written investment band within three working days.

03

Three weeks later

If you want to proceed, the rendered walkthrough film arrives — your kitchen, its four lighting states, the actual stone. Most clients watch it a dozen times before deciding.
