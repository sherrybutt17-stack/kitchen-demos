Questions

Money, without the theatre.

Including the resale question everyone asks and nobody answers honestly.

How much does a luxury kitchen remodel cost in Miami?

Obsidian commissions run $150,000 to $450,000. Roughly 35–45% goes to cabinetry, 15–25% to stone, 10–15% to lighting and electrical. Structural work, HOA compliance and hurricane-rated glazing account for most of the variance between projects.

Can you finance a luxury kitchen remodel?

Yes — fixed-rate terms from 60 to 180 months through private lending partners, plus construction-draw facilities above $250,000. Pre-qualification uses a soft credit pull and does not affect your credit score.

What is the payment schedule?

Five draws against verifiable milestones: 15% design agreement, 25% approved drawings, 25% cabinetry arriving in Miami, 25% stone installation, 10% punch-list completion.

Does a new kitchen add value to a Miami home?

In Coral Gables, Pinecrest and Key Biscayne a well-designed kitchen typically returns 60–75% of its cost at resale and materially shortens time on market. In high-rise condominiums the percentage return is usually lower, but the effect on saleability is stronger — renovated units in older towers are scarce.
