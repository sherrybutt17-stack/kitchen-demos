Six brands · four industries · 53 pages

Five websites. Five agencies.
One toolchain.

Each of these is a complete seven-page site with its own brand, positioning, customer, colour system, typography, layout philosophy and conversion strategy. No CSS, JavaScript or design token is shared between them — the divergence is the point. Three are kitchen remodeling, the fourth a security firm, and the fifth a real dental practice rebuilt from its own content — each one proving the pattern travels to another industry.

These cards open the source sites. The GoHighLevel-ready versions live in dist/ — same pages with all CSS, JavaScript and images inlined as base64, so each file pastes into a GHL Custom HTML block with nothing external to upload. Rebuild them any time with python3 scripts/build.py.

Site 05

SAPPHIREDental Studio, Winter Garden FL

A third industry, and the only one rebuilt from a real practice's own content. Porcelain light, cut-gem geometry, sapphire navy and champagne gold taken from the client's actual logo. Site 06

BLUE FOXLandscape & Services, Lake Mary FL

A fourth industry, and the second redesign of a real business from its own published material. Steel blue and pale ice measured straight off the client's logo — 77% and 17% of its opaque pixels — with the orange lifted from its favicon, and a serif display face answering the serif small-caps wordmark. Its centrepiece, The Property Plan, zones a lot by squarified treemap: every zone posts its own square footage and the total is the sum of the zones your selected services actually touch, so the drawing and the number cannot disagree. It deliberately quotes no price, because the company publishes none.

Market
Lake Mary & Central Florida
Customer
Homeowners building and maintaining
Type
Bitter · Source Sans 3
Feeling
Surveyed · built · kept
