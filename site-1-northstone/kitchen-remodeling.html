What is the difference between kitchen remodeling and kitchen renovation?

Renovation restores what is already there — new doors, new counters, the same footprint. Remodeling changes the plan itself: walls move, the work triangle is redrawn, and storage is rebuilt around how you actually cook.

Most firms will sell you whichever one they are better at. We tell you which one your house needs, and we recommend renovation whenever the existing layout genuinely works — it is the cheaper answer and sometimes it is the right one.

R1

Renovation

Same footprint. New cabinetry fronts or full replacement, new stone, new lighting and finishes. Typically 10–14 weeks on site and $45,000–$95,000. Right when the layout works and the materials have simply aged.

R2

Remodel

New plan. Walls, structure, windows and services move. Permitted, engineered and rebuilt around how the household actually uses the room. Typically 8–12 weeks on site after a 6–10 week design phase.

Custom cabinetry, milled for one house.

Stock cabinetry comes in three-inch increments and stops at eight feet. Your ceilings do not. We draw cabinetry to the room it is going into — full height, correct proportion, and fitted to what you actually own.

Everything is milled in our shop in north Scottsdale. That means we can change a drawer bank in week six without waiting on a distributor, and it means the person who cut the door is twenty minutes from your house if anything ever needs attention.

What is the best countertop material for a kitchen?

For most Arizona kitchens, quartzite offers the best balance — it carries the depth of natural marble but resists etching from citrus and heat far better. Choose marble if you want patina and accept it, porcelain for the largest uninterrupted runs, and engineered quartz if you want the lowest maintenance in a busy family kitchen.

Service 04

Do I need an architect for a kitchen remodel?

You need one whenever walls, rooflines, structural beams or window openings change — and for most kitchens worth remodeling, they should. The layout problem is almost always a structural problem wearing a decorating disguise.

Northstone includes architectural drafting in every project rather than subcontracting it. The person designing your layout is the same person who knows what the structure will allow — which is why our drawings do not change halfway through the build.

Questions

Before you book.

Plain answers to the four we hear most about remodeling specifically.

What does a kitchen remodel include?

A full Northstone remodel includes measured architectural drawings, structural coordination and permits, demolition, electrical and plumbing rework, custom cabinetry, stone fabrication and installation, tile, lighting, appliance integration, paint and final styling. One contract, one crew, one point of contact.

Can you remodel a kitchen without moving walls?

Yes — and roughly a third of our projects keep the existing footprint. If the layout already works, moving walls adds cost without adding function. We tell you which case you are in at the first consultation, before any drawings are commissioned.

How much do custom kitchen cabinets cost in Arizona?

Custom cabinetry in the Scottsdale market runs roughly $1,200 to $2,400 per linear foot depending on species, finish and interior fittings. A typical Northstone kitchen uses 28 to 42 linear feet. Because we mill in our own Arizona shop, you pay for the joinery rather than a distributor margin.

Will I be able to use my kitchen during construction?

Not the room itself — but we set up a temporary kitchen with your refrigerator, microwave and a working sink in an adjacent space on day one, at no charge. Dust walls with zippered access go up before the first cabinet comes out.
