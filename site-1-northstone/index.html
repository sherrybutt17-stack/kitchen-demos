The problem

Why most new kitchens feel old in three years.

A kitchen fails long before the finishes date. It fails on the drawing — in the decisions nobody questioned, made by people who never watched you cook in the room.

01

It was ordered, not drawn

Catalogue boxes arranged to fit a wall. The house was never consulted — so the proportions fight the ceiling height, and the room always feels slightly borrowed.

02

The layout was never questioned

The sink stayed where the plumber found it in 1998. Every trip from fridge to range still crosses the one place three people stand.

03

Storage came last

Cabinets sized to the leftover space rather than to what you own. Two years on, the island is a landing strip and the pantry is chaos.

04

Materials were chosen on a screen

A slab picked from a thumbnail behaves nothing like the one in your light. Veining lands in the wrong place, and the seam sits where you look every morning.

Questions

Answered plainly.

The six things people ask before they book — with real numbers, not ranges designed to avoid the question.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Scottsdale?

A full architectural kitchen remodel in Scottsdale and Paradise Valley typically runs $85,000 to $250,000. The range is driven by three things: whether walls or structure move, whether cabinetry is custom-built or modified stock, and the stone you select. Northstone provides a fixed written investment range at the end of the first consultation, before any drawings begin.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Plan on five to seven months end to end. Design and drawings take six to ten weeks, custom cabinetry is milled in ten to fourteen weeks, and on-site construction runs eight to twelve weeks. Northstone sequences cabinetry milling to run alongside design approval, which removes roughly a month from the traditional timeline.

Do I need an architect for a kitchen remodel?

You need one whenever walls, rooflines, structural beams or window openings change — and for most kitchens worth remodeling, they should. Northstone includes architectural drafting in every project rather than subcontracting it, so the person designing your layout is the same person who understands what the structure will allow.

What is the difference between kitchen remodeling and kitchen renovation?

Renovation restores what is already there — new doors, new counters, same footprint. Remodeling changes the plan itself: walls move, the work triangle is redrawn, storage is rebuilt around how you actually cook. Northstone does both, but only recommends renovation when the existing layout genuinely works.

Do you offer financing for kitchen remodeling?

Yes. Northstone offers 0% APR for 18 months on approved credit, and fixed-rate terms from 60 to 144 months through our lending partners. Applications take about four minutes and use a soft credit pull, so checking your rate does not affect your credit score. See the full financing page →
