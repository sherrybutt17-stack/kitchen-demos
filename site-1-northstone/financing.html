Questions

Money questions, answered straight.

Nobody enjoys this part. Here is everything you would otherwise have to ask twice.

Can you finance a kitchen remodel?

Yes. Northstone offers 0% APR for 18 months on approved credit, and fixed-rate terms from 60 to 144 months through our lending partners. The application takes about four minutes and uses a soft credit pull, so checking your rate does not affect your credit score.

Does checking my rate hurt my credit score?

No. Pre-qualification uses a soft credit inquiry, which is not visible to other lenders and has no effect on your score. A hard inquiry only happens if you accept an offer and choose to move forward.

What credit score do I need to finance a kitchen remodel?

Most approvals come in above 660, and the 0% promotional tier generally requires 700 or higher. Applicants below that range are frequently approved on longer fixed-rate terms, so it is worth checking rather than assuming.

Is a HELOC better than contractor financing?

A HELOC usually carries a lower rate because your home secures it, but it takes three to six weeks to close and places a lien on the property. Contractor financing is unsecured and funds in days. Many clients use the 0% promotional period for the deposit and a HELOC for the balance.

Does financing change the price of the work?

No. The estimate is the estimate. We take no commission from any lender, and there is no cash discount — which also means there is no financing surcharge hidden in the bid.
