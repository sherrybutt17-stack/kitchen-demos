What this is

You approve a picture of your own property, not a paragraph.

A member of our professional landscape design team will meet with you to get your vision, then create a beautiful rendering of the finished product.

That step exists because of the single most expensive failure in this trade: finding out you did not want it once it is in the ground. Plants are living things on a schedule — moving a specimen tree after planting is not an edit, it is a second job. Seeing the finished scheme first is cheap by comparison.

About the design fee. Estimates are free. The only published fee is digital landscape design work: as of 7/1/2023 it is $250, with a 50% deposit, and the $250 is credited toward the landscape project if the package is approved.

What's involved

How it works, and what shapes the quote.

Residential and commercial, across Central Florida — Ocoee, Winter Garden, Windermere, Winter Park, Lake Mary, Heathrow and Apopka.

01

We meet, and you talk

A member of the design team walks the property with you and listens to what you want it to be.

02

You see a rendering

A rendering of the finished landscape, so you approve what it will look like before anything is dug.

03

We build it

Installation by the same company that designed it — sod, pavers, irrigation, drainage, trees and lighting under one crew.

What affects the scope

  • The size of the area being redesigned
  • What has to come out before anything goes in
  • Hardscape — pavers, edging, rock and gravel
  • New irrigation, or changes to the existing system
  • Drainage, if the property holds water
  • Lighting, if you want the landscape to read after dark

Every quote is based on the property itself — the estimate visit is where the measuring happens, and it is free.

Questions

Before you call.

Why does the digital design cost $250 when the estimate is free?

Because they are different things. An estimate is us pricing work we can see. A digital design is a designer producing a rendering of your property. As of 7/1/2023 that work costs $250 with a 50% deposit — and if the landscape package is approved, the $250 is credited toward the project.

Can I have the design without the build?

Yes. The $250 credit only applies if the landscape package goes ahead with us; that is the only difference.

Do you do commercial landscape construction?

Yes — residential and commercial, across Central Florida, including Ocoee, Winter Garden, Windermere, Winter Park, Lake Mary, Heathrow and Apopka.

Can the work be done in phases?

Yes. Many properties start with one area and add the rest later. Tell us your priorities and budget on the estimate visit and the plan can be split accordingly.
