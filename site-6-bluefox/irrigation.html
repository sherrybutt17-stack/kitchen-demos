What this is

Irrigation in Lake Mary and the surrounding areas.

The expert irrigation installers at Blue Fox Landscape & Services in Lake Mary, FL, and the surrounding area can install your irrigation system to keep your plants thriving all year long. We can also repair and maintain irrigation systems to ensure they're working at an optimum level.

Irrigation is the quietest way to lose money on a landscape. A head that has drifted out of alignment waters a fence for six years; a cracked lateral waters the ground under a driveway. You do not see either one — you see a dead patch somewhere else and assume the plant was the problem.

What's involved

Three things worth knowing.

Serving Lake Mary, Heathrow, Altamonte Springs, Longwood and Orlando, Florida — residential and commercial.

01

Installation

New systems for residential and commercial properties, laid out for what is actually planted rather than a uniform grid.

02

Repair

Heads, lines, valves and controllers. Usually cheaper than the water bill it has been quietly inflating.

03

Maintenance

Checked and adjusted so it keeps working at an optimum level, rather than degrading a season at a time.

Questions

Before you call.

My lawn has dead patches but the system runs. What now?

That is the classic symptom of coverage rather than supply — a head blocked by growth, knocked out of arc, or under-pressured at the end of a zone. It is a repair visit, not a new system.

Can you take over a system somebody else installed?

Yes. We repair and maintain existing irrigation systems as well as installing new ones.

Do you handle commercial irrigation?

Yes, residential and commercial both.
