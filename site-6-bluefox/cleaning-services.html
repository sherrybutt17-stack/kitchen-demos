The rate card

What does house cleaning cost in north Phoenix?

Blue Fox charges an hourly rate for a two-person crew, and publishes it. A house that is kept up costs less than one that is not, because it takes less time — which is exactly what an hourly rate is for and exactly what a flat quote hides.

Why hourly and not a flat price

A flat price has to assume the worst house it might land on, or it has to be renegotiated on the doorstep. Hourly means a well-kept home genuinely pays less, and it means we have no incentive to rush the last room. The trade is that you want a reliable estimate up front — so we built one.
