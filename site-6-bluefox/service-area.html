Where we clean

Four towns. That is the whole map.

Cave Creek, Anthem, Carefree and North Scottsdale, plus New River. We could cover more of the Valley. We deliberately do not — and the reason is the two promises we would have to break first.

The reason for the radius

A big service area is how "same crew, on time" quietly stops being true.

Two things we promise depend entirely on geography. The first is that we arrive when we said. The second is that you get the same two people each visit. Both survive a tight radius and neither survives a wide one — a crew that finishes in Chandler cannot be in Carefree at eleven, so the schedule starts getting rebuilt around driving instead of around customers, and the crew you had last month is wherever the gap was this month.

So we grow the density inside four towns rather than the size of the map. If you are just outside it, call anyway — we will tell you honestly whether we can serve you properly rather than book you and hope.
