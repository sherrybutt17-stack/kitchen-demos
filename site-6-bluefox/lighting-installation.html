What this is

Landscape lighting in Lake Mary and the surrounding areas.

Blue Fox Landscape & Services installs exterior landscape lighting as part of our landscape design and construction work, or on its own for a property whose planting is already where you want it.

Lighting is the cheapest square-foot upgrade in landscaping and the one most often skipped. A paver walkway, a specimen oak and a clean facade are all things you bought and then only see in daylight. Uplighting a trunk and grazing a wall costs a fraction of what either of them cost to build.

What's involved

Three things worth knowing.

Serving Lake Mary, Heathrow, Altamonte Springs, Longwood and Orlando, Florida — residential and commercial.

01

Path and walkway

So the route from the kerb to the door reads at night, which is when most guests actually use it.

02

Uplighting and specimen trees

The single highest-impact fixture on most Central Florida lots, because the canopies are worth looking at.

03

Facade and feature

Grazing light across a wall, a water feature or a paver field — the things a flat porch light flattens.

Questions

Before you call.

Can lighting be added to a landscape you did not install?

Yes. It is often the fastest way to improve a property that is already well planted.

It can be. Exterior lighting is one of the features our landscape design and construction team designs and installs.
