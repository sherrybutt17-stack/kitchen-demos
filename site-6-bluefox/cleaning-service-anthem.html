Why Anthem is its own job

Anthem houses are newer, busier, and cleaned around a schedule rather than a crisis.

Anthem is master-planned and largely built after 1999, which means tighter envelopes, less grit than Cave Creek, and floor plans that repeat. The pressure here is not dirt, it is calendar: two working parents, school runs off Anthem Way, sport at the Community Park, and a house that is fine on Tuesday and gone by Thursday. What those households buy from us is a fixed slot that does not move and a crew that does not change.

Which is also why the 48-hour free reschedule matters more here than anywhere else we work. Life in Anthem moves; the booking should be able to move with it, without a fee.
