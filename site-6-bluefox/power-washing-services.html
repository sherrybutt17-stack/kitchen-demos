What this is

Power washing in Lake Mary, FL and the surrounding areas.

Pressure washing takes something that looks like it needs replacing and makes it look new again — driveways, walkways, patios, decks, fences and gates, at homes and commercial properties.

It is also the service where the difference between operators shows afterwards. Lap marks on a driveway, a striped fence and etched concrete all come from rushing — and they are permanent.

Questions

Before you call.

What is the difference between power washing and soft washing?

Pressure. Power washing uses high-pressure water on hard surfaces that can take it — concrete, pavers, some decking. Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution for surfaces that cannot — roofs, siding, gutters. We treat them as two separate services.
