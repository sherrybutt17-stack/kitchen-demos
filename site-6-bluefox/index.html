Questions

The ones people ask before they call.

Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. There is an after-hours number for anything that will not keep.

What areas do you serve?

Lake Mary, Heathrow, Altamonte Springs, Longwood and Orlando, Florida — plus Winter Park, Winter Garden, Windermere and Ocoee, where we confirm availability for your address first. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm; call 407-717-8191, or 407-969-7137 after hours.

Are your estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are free. The single exception is digital landscape design work, which as of 7/1/2023 costs $250 with a 50% deposit — and if the landscape package is approved, that $250 is credited toward the landscape project.

What is the difference between power washing and soft washing?

Power washing uses high-pressure water on hard surfaces that can take it — concrete driveways, walkways, patios. Soft washing uses low-pressure water with a mixture of cleaning solutions for surfaces that cannot — roofs, siding, gutters. A roof should never see a pressure washer, which is why the two are separate services.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. Every service is available residential and commercial, from single-family homes to commercial buildings and whole communities.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — through Acorn Finance, where you can apply and get payment options from multiple lenders. Checking your options does not commit you to anything.

How does the landscape design process work?

A member of our professional landscape design team meets with you to get your vision, then creates a rendering of the finished product. You approve a picture of your own property before any ground is broken.

Can you do landscaping and washing on the same property?

Yes — that is the point of one company. A new landscape and a clean driveway, house and roof can be scoped together, and the same team is there to keep it looking new afterwards.
