Got questions?

The answers, including the ones about money.

Every policy below is the one we actually operate, written the way it is enforced — not a softened version you find out about later.

What's the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A deep clean is recommended for every first clean by Blue Fox. It buys the crew extra time for the areas regular cleanings have been missing — ceiling fans, light fixtures, vents, woodwork, hand-wiped baseboards and the range hood inside and out. After that initial deep clean, most customers switch to a standard two-hour clean and stay there.

How do I book?

Click Get a free quote and enter your information. A team member will contact you to solidify the booking. No appointment is officially scheduled until we have reached out — so nothing is confirmed, and nothing is charged, before you have spoken to a person.

What types of products do you use?

We use all natural cleaning products in every home. Occasionally, in bathrooms, we use harsher agents to ensure sanitation. Every bottle, powder and cloth we carry is listed by brand name rather than summarised as "eco-friendly".

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. We provide all of the cleaning supplies, including vacuums, mops and cleaning agents. You are never billed for materials and you never need to keep anything in the house for us.

Are the cleaners the same each time?

Yes — we do our best to make sure the cleaners stay on your schedule. It is the single hardest promise in this industry to keep, and the only reason we can keep it is that people stay: Blue Fox pays some of the highest wages in the trade and promotes from within.

Cancellations and refunds

If you cancel more than 48 hours ahead you receive a full refund to the payment information on file. If you cancel within 48 hours of your booking, it is credited to your account to use whenever you'd like. If you cancel within 24 hours, it is a non-refundable full charge.

Is there a rescheduling fee?

No. We do not charge for rescheduling as long as it is done more than 48 hours before your original booking.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most of our recurring customers are at work when we clean. Tell us how you'd like us to get in when you book, and the same crew uses the same arrangement each visit.
