Why one company

Most yards are not badly designed. They are badly maintained.

A landscape is finished on the day the crew leaves and starts declining the morning after. The pavers grey over, the roof streaks, the irrigation drifts out of alignment, the hedges lose their line — and two years later the property looks like the money was never spent.

Blue Fox does both halves. We design and construct the landscape, and we run the power washing and soft washing that keeps it — and the house it sits against — looking new. That is unusual: most landscapers do not wash, and most washing companies cannot plant. Having one company on both means nobody gets to blame the other.

Keeping it that way

What is the difference between power washing and soft washing?

Pressure. Power washing uses high-pressure water on hard surfaces that can take it. Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution for surfaces that cannot. The method follows the surface — and getting that backwards is how exteriors get wrecked.

High pressure · hard surfaces

Power washing

Driveways and walkways, patios and decks, fences and gates. Removes dirt, stains and grime from residential and commercial properties.

Power washing services Low pressure · everything else

Soft washing

Roof cleaning, house wash and gutters. Low-pressure water with cleaning solutions to remove molds, mildew, dirt and grime from the exterior of homes.

What is the difference between power washing and soft washing?

Power washing uses high-pressure water on hard surfaces that can take it — concrete driveways, walkways, patios. Soft washing uses low-pressure water with a mixture of cleaning solutions for surfaces that cannot — roofs, siding, gutters. A roof should never see a pressure washer, which is why we keep the two as separate services rather than one hose.
