What this is

Paver Sand & Seal across Central Florida.

A pressure wash makes pavers clean. Paver Sand & Seal is what sets them up to stay that way: once the surface is washed, the joints between the pavers are filled with fresh sand and the whole surface is sealed.

The sand in a paver joint is what holds the field together. When it washes out, pavers start to move and weeds find the gaps. Refilling the joints and sealing over them helps stabilise the pavers and reduce weed growth — which is why it is the upgrade most worth adding to a paver wash.

Questions

Before you call.

What is Paver Sand & Seal?

It is the step after a pressure wash. The pavers are washed clean, the joints between them are filled with fresh sand, and the surface is sealed. Sealing over re-sanded joints helps stabilise the pavers and reduce weed growth between them.

How is it different from pressure washing?

A pressure wash cleans the surface and stops there. Paver Sand & Seal includes the wash, then restores the joint sand and seals the pavers — it is the difference between a clean driveway and a driveway set up to stay that way.

Can you fix sunken or shifted pavers first?

Yes. Pavers that have sunk, shifted or lifted can be taken up and relaid before the sand and seal — ask for both in the same quote.

How is it priced, and is the estimate free?

By the square foot, because the size of the area is what drives the work. The estimate is free and it is where the measuring happens.
