What this is

Roof cleaning in Lake Mary and the surrounding areas.

Blue Fox Landscape & Services provides fast, friendly customer service and expert roof cleanings, using our soft washing technique, making your roof look new again. Our soft washing specialists will provide you with a thorough roof cleaning ensuring your roof is completely cleared of molds, grime, dirt, and more.

The important word there is soft. A roof is the one surface on a Florida property that must never see a pressure washer — high pressure lifts granules off asphalt shingles, drives water up under the courses, and turns a cosmetic problem into a roofing bill. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution, so the growth dies and the roof does not.

Questions

Before you call.

Why not just pressure wash the roof?

Because high pressure strips the granules that protect asphalt shingles and can force water under the courses. It looks fine for a season and shortens the life of the roof. Soft washing is the correct method and it is the only one we use up there.

What actually causes the black streaks?

Biological growth — mold, mildew and algae — not road dirt. That is why it comes back after a rinse and why a cleaning solution rather than force is what removes it.

Do you clean gutters at the same time?

Yes, soft wash gutters are part of our soft washing services and it makes obvious sense to do them on the same visit.
