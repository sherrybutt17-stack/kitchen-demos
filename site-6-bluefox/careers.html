Employees come first

This industry has a turnover problem. We decided to pay our way out of it.

Cleaning is hard, physical, unglamorous work, and most companies in it treat people as interchangeable. Then they wonder why nobody stays, why customers complain that a different person turns up every month, and why the good ones leave first.

Blue Fox pays some of the highest wages throughout all of North America for this work. Not because we are generous — because taking care of our team produces the best possible outcome, and every promise we make to a customer runs through somebody staying long enough to keep it.

You will never be asked to buy your own supplies, drive your own car to a job, or work a house on your own. Crews are two people. Always.

Your opportunity

Housekeeper / home cleaner

Blue Fox Cleaning is seeking a reliable and detail-oriented home cleaner to join our team, working in Cave Creek, New River and Anthem, AZ.

What you'll do

  • Provide high-quality cleaning services to clients, including dusting, vacuuming and cleaning surfaces
  • Pay attention to detail and ensure that all areas of the space are cleaned
  • Communicate with clients to address any concerns or special requests
  • Maintain a clean and organised work area
  • Work as one half of a two-person crew — never alone

What you'll need

  • Experience in cleaning — not required, we train
  • Ability to work independently with minimal supervision
  • Attention to detail
  • Willingness to learn our eco-friendly cleaning products
  • Good communication skills
  • Reliability and punctuality
