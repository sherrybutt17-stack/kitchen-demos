Questions

Before you call.

Do you clean windows inside and out?

Yes. Exterior window cleaning covers the outside glass, frames and sills; interior window cleaning covers the inside glass and sills. Book either one on its own, or both together.

Can I book window cleaning on a schedule?

Yes — as a one-time clean, or on a monthly or quarterly schedule.

How is window cleaning priced?

By the pane, so the quote depends on how many windows the property has and which schedule you choose. The estimate is free.

Can I add window cleaning to a house wash or pressure wash?

Yes. Windows are commonly booked with a house wash, a gutter clean or a driveway, and scoping them together is usually the most efficient way to do it.

Do you clean windows at commercial properties?

Yes — residential and commercial.
