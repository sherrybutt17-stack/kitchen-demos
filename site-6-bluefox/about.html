Our mission

Blue Fox is not about cleaning at all.

It is about the happiness that comes with it. That sounds like a slogan until you hear where it came from, which is a kitchen in the dark at six in the morning.

The founder

A clean kitchen improved everyone's mood. That was the whole discovery.

Laura started getting up at six before school to clean the kitchen. Not because anyone asked her to, and not because she liked cleaning. She had noticed something: her mother came home to a clean kitchen in a different mood than she came home to a dirty one. Same job, same day, same commute — different evening.

That is a strange thing for a child to work out, and an obvious thing to build a company on once you have. Blue Fox exists because a clean house is not the point. The point is the person who walks into it at six in the evening, and what the next four hours of their life are like. The cleaning is just the mechanism.

The plan was never modest, either: build the best cleaning company the world has ever seen, with friends. It is still the plan. Four towns is where it starts, not where it ends.

“Blue Fox isn't about cleaning at all. Blue Fox is about the happiness that comes with it.”

Laura — founder, Blue Fox Cleaning

Est. 2020 · Phoenix, Arizona

The team comes first

The reason we can promise you the same crew.

Every cleaning company promises consistency. Almost none deliver it, and the reason is not scheduling software — it is turnover. You cannot send the same two people back to a house for two years if those two people leave after five months.

So we took the obvious route rather than the clever one. Blue Fox pays some of the highest wages throughout all of North America for this work. We provide the vehicles, the uniforms and every supply, so nobody is subsidising their own job. Crews are two people, never one, because cleaning alone all day is how good people burn out. And we promote from within.

Taking care of our team produces the best possible outcome. Not as a value on a wall — as the mechanism by which the person who cleaned your house in March is the person who cleans it in September.
