Landscape financing

A landscape is a capital project. Pay for it like one.

We work with Acorn Finance, where you can apply and get affordable payment options from multiple lenders — several offers side by side, rather than one number to take or leave.

Check my options at Acorn Get an estimate first

How it works

Get the estimate first. Then decide how to pay for it.

Financing is easier to think about when you know the number. Estimates are free, so the sensible order is: we come and look, you get a real figure, and then you decide whether you would rather pay it at once or over time.

Acorn is a marketplace rather than a single lender, which is the part worth knowing. You submit once and see what several lenders will actually offer you, instead of applying to one and hoping. Checking your options does not commit you to the project, and it does not commit you to us.

Three steps

  • Get your free estimate from Blue Fox
  • Apply once through Acorn Finance
  • Compare the offers that come back and choose

Rates, terms and amounts are set by the lenders, not by us — so we do not quote them here. Acorn shows you what you actually qualify for.

Worth saying plainly

The one fee we charge, and when you get it back.

Estimates are free. As of 7/1/2023 our landscape digital design work is not — it is $250.00, with a 50% deposit required for digital design work. If the landscape package is approved, the $250.00 is credited toward the landscape project. That is the whole fee structure, and it is on this page so nobody hears it for the first time on a call.

Questions

About paying for the work.

Does applying affect my credit?

That is Acorn's process rather than ours, and they explain it on their own application — which is exactly why we link out to them rather than describe their terms second-hand here.

Do I have to finance through you?

No. Acorn is an option we offer because customers ask for one. Pay however suits you.

Can I finance a washing job as well as landscaping?

Ask us. Financing generally makes sense on construction-scale work; a single pressure-washing visit usually does not need it.

Is the $250 design fee financeable?

Raise it on the estimate call. Remember it comes back to you as credit against the project if the landscape package is approved.
