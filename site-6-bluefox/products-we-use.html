Why this page exists

"All-natural" is a marketing word until somebody names the bottle.

Every cleaning company in Arizona says it uses natural products. Almost none of them will tell you which ones, because the moment you publish a list you can be held to it — by a customer with an allergy, by a parent, by anyone with a stone counter and a sealer warranty.

We publish it anyway. If a product on this list is wrong for your house, you will know before we arrive rather than after, and we will use yours on that surface instead.

The practical consequence

Nothing on this list needs anyone to leave the room.

That is the actual reason we buy these products, not the marketing reason. There are no dwell times to observe, no ventilation windows, no "keep off it until it dries". A dog crossing a floor four minutes after we mop it is fine, and so is a toddler.

100% pet friendly, 100% of the time. Tell us their names when you book and the crew that comes back next time will already know them.

Questions

About what we use.

Are your cleaning products safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use all natural cleaning products in every home, chosen so that a child on a floor or a dog crossing it four minutes after we mop is a non-event. There are no dwell times to observe and nobody needs to leave the house. The one exception is bathrooms, where we occasionally use a harsher agent to guarantee sanitation — and it is used there and nowhere else.

Do I need to supply anything?

No. We provide all of the cleaning supplies, including vacuums, mops and cleaning agents. Nothing on this page is billed to you as materials — it is all covered by the hourly rate.

Can you use my products instead?

Yes. Stone sealers and specialist wood finishes are the usual reasons somebody asks. Leave the product out with a note, tell us when you book, and the crew will use yours on that surface and ours everywhere else.

Someone in the house has an allergy or is chemically sensitive. What now?

Tell us before the first visit. The list above is exactly why we publish it — you can check every ingredient against what you need to avoid in your own time, and we can substitute or drop a product rather than discovering the problem in your home.
