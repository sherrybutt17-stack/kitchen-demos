Before you call

The three we get asked most.

How do I book?

Send the free quote form or call. A team member will contact you to solidify the booking. No appointment is officially scheduled until we reach out — so nothing is confirmed and nothing is charged before you have spoken to a person.

What does it cost?

$100/hr for a basic clean, $110 for a deep clean and $115 for a move in/out, in every case for a two-person crew. Those are published, not quoted. The Walkthrough will estimate the hours for your room count before you call.

What if I need to cancel or move it?

Rescheduling is free as long as it is more than 48 hours before the booking. Cancel more than 48 hours ahead and you get a full refund; within 48 hours it is credited to your account to use whenever you like; within 24 hours it is a non-refundable full charge.
