Honest limits

What is not on the list.

Published checklists are only worth anything if they are also a boundary. These are the jobs a basic clean does not include as standard. None of them is a refusal — most are things we will happily quote — but you should not discover that on the day.

Ask when you book and we will tell you the time it adds before you commit to it.

Not included as standard

  • Inside the oven and inside the refrigerator
  • Exterior windows
  • Laundry, or dishes beyond loading the dishwasher
  • Anything needing a ladder above standard reach
  • Garages, patios and outdoor living areas

When to book it

Is this the one I need?

Three ways this clean is normally used. If none of them sounds like your house, say so on the phone — we would rather move you to the right one than sell you this one.

01

Homes already on a rhythm

You had a deep clean, or you keep on top of it yourself, and you want it held there rather than rescued.

02

Every two weeks, mostly

Most of our customers land on bi-weekly. Weekly and monthly both work; the difference shows up in how long each visit takes.

03

Around two hours, typically

A maintained two-bed, two-bath usually runs about two hours for the crew. Bigger or busier houses run longer, and you are billed for what it takes.

Questions

About the basic clean.

More answers — including the cancellation policy in full — are on the homepage.

How long does a basic clean take?

A maintained two-bed, two-bath is usually around two hours for a two-person crew. Add roughly twenty-five minutes for each additional bedroom and bathroom pair. The Walkthrough on the homepage will price your exact room count against the same figures.

Can I add something to the list?

Yes. Put it in the notes when you book, or tell the crew. Anything outside the published list is quoted before it is done, never after.

Do I have to tidy up first?

No. Picking up clutter means the crew spends its time cleaning rather than sorting, which usually makes the visit shorter — but it is your call, not a requirement.
