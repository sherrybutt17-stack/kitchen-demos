About Blue Fox in Orlando

Local landscaping and exterior cleaning.

Blue Fox Landscape & Services provides local landscape design & construction, pressure washing, and soft washing services to the Orlando, FL community. Our trained and experienced landscape designers can transform your landscaping ideas into realities. We install irrigation, driveways, patios and more. We also offer professional pressure washing for stubborn grease or dirt stains on driveways, walkways, decks, patios, fences and gates, and soft washing to remove mold, mildew, dirt and grime from your home's exterior, roofing and gutters.

Questions

Before you call.

Do you serve Orlando?

Yes. Orlando is one of the five communities in our established service area, for residential and commercial properties.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Estimates are free. The only published fee is digital landscape design work: as of 7/1/2023 it is $250, with a 50% deposit, and the $250 is credited toward the landscape project if the package is approved.

Can you do landscaping and washing on the same property?

Yes. Both halves are handled by one company, so they can be scoped and scheduled together.
