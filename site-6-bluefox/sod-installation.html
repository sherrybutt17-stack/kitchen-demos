Sod installation

Does your lawn look dead with large areas of brown patches? If so, contact the sod installation experts at Blue Fox Landscape & Services.

What this is

Sod installation in Lake Mary and the surrounding areas.

One of our highly trained landscapers will come out to your property and evaluate the condition of your lawn, assisting you with determining if a new sod installation is the best course of action to elevate the aesthetics and health of your lawn.

That evaluation is the part worth having. Not every brown lawn needs sod — some need water, some need a drainage fix, and some need a shade-tolerant variety rather than the one that was laid last time. Replacing turf that was only ever going to fail again is the most expensive mistake in this trade, and it is avoidable in one visit.

Questions

Before you call.

How do I know if I need new sod or just better watering?

That is exactly what the evaluation is for. Large dead patches with healthy grass between them usually point at irrigation coverage or a drainage problem; a lawn that is uniformly thin and brown is more often a sod question. We would rather sell you an irrigation repair than sod you do not need.

Do you install sod for commercial properties?

Yes. We offer both commercial and residential sod installations across Lake Mary, Heathrow, Altamonte Springs, Longwood and Orlando.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Estimates are free. The only thing that carries a fee is our digital landscape design work, which is $250 and is credited back toward the project if the landscape package is approved.
