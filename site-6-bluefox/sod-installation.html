Sod installation

Does your lawn look dead with large areas of brown patches? If so, contact the sod installation experts at BlueFox Landscape & Services.

Free estimatesResidential & commercialCentral Florida
Get a free quote Call 407-717-8191

What this is

Sod installation across Central Florida.

One of our highly trained landscapers will come out to your property and evaluate the condition of your lawn, assisting you with determining if a new sod installation is the best course of action to elevate the aesthetics and health of your lawn.

That evaluation is the part worth having. Not every brown lawn needs sod — some need water, some need a drainage fix, and some need a shade-tolerant variety rather than the one that was laid last time. Replacing turf that was only ever going to fail again is avoidable in one visit.

What's involved

How it works, and what shapes the quote.

Residential and commercial, across Central Florida — Ocoee, Winter Garden, Windermere, Winter Park, Lake Mary, Heathrow and Apopka.

01

We look before we quote

A landscaper walks the lawn and tells you whether sod is actually the answer. Sometimes it is irrigation. Sometimes it is drainage.

02

Residential and commercial

We offer both commercial and residential sod installations, at whatever scale the property needs.

03

Prepped, not just laid

Sod put straight onto compacted or dead ground fails the same way the last lot did. Preparation is what you are paying for.

What affects the scope

  • Square footage of turf being replaced
  • Removing the old lawn and grading the ground
  • Sun and shade across the lawn
  • Whether the irrigation covers the new sod properly
  • Low spots where water sits after rain
  • Access for pallets and equipment

Every quote is based on the property itself — the estimate visit is where the measuring happens, and it is free.

Questions

Before you call.

How do I know if I need new sod or just better watering?

That is exactly what the evaluation is for. Large dead patches with healthy grass between them usually point at irrigation coverage or a drainage problem; a lawn that is uniformly thin and brown is more often a sod question.

Do you install sod for commercial properties?

Yes. We offer both commercial and residential sod installations.
