Every exterior surface

Pressure washing and soft washing, each where it belongs.

The method follows the surface. Concrete and pavers take high pressure; roofs, siding and gutters do not, and are soft washed with low pressure and a cleaning solution instead. Getting that backwards is how exteriors get damaged, which is why each service here is scoped separately.

Two methods

What is the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

The amount of pressure. Pressure washing uses high-pressure water on hard surfaces that can take it. Soft washing uses low pressure plus a cleaning solution for surfaces that cannot.

Questions

Before you call.

Can I book several services on one visit?

Yes. Driveway, house, gutters, roof, solar panels and windows are commonly booked together, and it is usually the most efficient way to do it.

Will you pressure wash my roof?

No. Roofs are soft washed only — high pressure strips shingle granules and can force water under the courses.

Do you wash commercial properties?

Yes — residential and commercial, from single homes to buildings and whole communities.
