Our promise to your family

The Kitchen-in-Use Promise.

Before a single cabinet comes out, we build you a temporary kitchen — a working sink, your refrigerator, a microwave and counter space, in whatever room suits your house. It stays until the new kitchen is genuinely usable.

It is written into every contract, it costs you nothing extra, and in nineteen years we have never once skipped it. Four hundred and thirty families have cooked dinner at home through their own remodel.

How it goes

Five steps, and you always know which one you're in.

Nobody enjoys not knowing what happens next. So we tell you, in writing, before anything starts.

1

An evening at your table

Ninety minutes in your kitchen around dinner time, on purpose. We watch where the bottleneck is, where homework lands, who reaches over whom. Free, and you keep the written range we leave behind.

2

Design you can walk through

Full plans plus a 3D walkthrough you can send to your mother. We revise until it is right — there is no revision limit, because a kitchen you half-like is a bad outcome for both of us.

3

Selections, in one afternoon

Cabinetry, stone, tile, hardware and paint chosen together at the studio with everything laid out at once. No Saturday showroom marathons, no decisions made under pressure.

4

Build — with your kitchen still working

Temporary kitchen in, dust walls sealed, weekly written schedule on the fridge. One project lead, reachable by text, who is on your site every day.

5

The first dinner

We clean, style and photograph the room, then leave. Punch list closed within ten days, and we call at six months and again at two years to check how it is actually living.

The studio

We are a family business that builds kitchens for families.

Hearth & Hollis began in 2007 when Ana Hollis, a cabinetmaker, and her brother-in-law Sam Hearth, a contractor, got tired of watching Marin families endure remodels that treated them as an obstacle.

Nineteen years later the shop is still on Miller Avenue, Ana still signs off on every cabinet drawing, and we still cap the calendar so no family ever competes for attention. Our crew has been together an average of nine years — the person who tiles your backsplash has tiled four hundred before it.

Questions

Everything families ask us first.

Real numbers, real timelines. If we don't know, we say so.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Marin County?

Most Marin County kitchen remodels land between $65,000 and $180,000. A refresh keeping the existing layout usually runs $65,000–$95,000, while opening the kitchen to a family room and rebuilding storage runs $110,000–$180,000. We give every family a written range at the first visit, free.

Can we live at home during a kitchen remodel?

Yes, and almost every family does. Our Kitchen-in-Use Promise means you keep a working sink, your refrigerator and a microwave in a temporary kitchen from day one until the new room is ready. We set it up before demolition, at no extra cost.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Four to six months from first sketch to finished room — five to eight weeks of design and selections, eight to twelve weeks building cabinetry, six to ten weeks on site. You get a written weekly schedule so you always know who is in your house and when.

Do you help with the design, or do we need our own designer?

Design is included. Every project starts with a designer who spends an evening watching how your family actually uses the kitchen — where homework happens, where the dog bowl lives, who cooks together. You are also welcome to bring your own designer; we work alongside them happily.

Do you offer financing for kitchen remodeling?

Yes — 0% APR for 12 months on approved credit, and fixed terms up to 120 months. Checking your rate takes about three minutes and uses a soft credit pull, so it does not affect your credit score. See financing →

Which areas of Marin do you serve?

We work throughout Mill Valley, Tiburon, Belvedere, Larkspur, Corte Madera, Sausalito, Ross, Kentfield and San Rafael. Our studio is on Miller Avenue in Mill Valley, so most projects sit within fifteen minutes of the shop. See service areas →
