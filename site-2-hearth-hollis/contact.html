Service areas

Where does Hearth & Hollis work?

We work throughout Mill Valley, Tiburon, Belvedere, Larkspur, Corte Madera, Sausalito, Ross, Kentfield and San Rafael, California — and almost every project sits within fifteen minutes of the shop.

That radius is deliberate. Our project lead visits every active site daily, and cabinetry adjustments come from a workshop you could drive to in your slippers.

01

Mill Valley

Our home town. Sycamore Park, Homestead Valley, Tam Valley and Cascade Canyon — mostly mid-century houses with kitchens that were never meant to be shared.

02

Tiburon & Belvedere

Hillside homes where the kitchen usually has the best view in the house and almost never uses it. That is normally the first thing we change.

03

Larkspur & Corte Madera

Family neighbourhoods, school runs, and kitchens that need to survive three children and a labrador. Our busiest bracket by some distance.

04

Sausalito

Tight footprints and tricky access. Small kitchens reward good design more than large ones do — there is nowhere for a mistake to hide.

05

Ross & Kentfield

Older, larger houses where the kitchen was originally built for staff. Opening those up is some of the most satisfying work we do.

06

San Rafael

From Dominican to Terra Linda. A broad mix, and the area where a renovation rather than a full remodel is most often the honest answer.
