Should we remodel, or just renovate?

Renovate when the layout already works and only the surfaces have aged. Remodel when the room actively fights your family — when two people cannot pass, when there is nowhere to land a schoolbag, when the fridge door blocks the only walkway.

What we build · 03

What is the best countertop for a family kitchen?

For households with children, engineered quartz is usually the right answer. It does not etch from juice or tomato, never needs sealing, and survives homework, glitter and hot pans without a story attached.

Choose quartzite if you want real stone that still tolerates real life. Keep marble for a baking zone rather than the whole run — it is beautiful and it will patina, and families who love it are the ones who were told that honestly up front. Butcher block on an island end is the warmest thing you can do to a kitchen for very little money.

Questions

About remodeling specifically.

The four we are asked most once families get past the price.

What is included in a kitchen remodel?

Design and 3D walkthroughs, permits, the temporary kitchen setup, demolition, electrical and plumbing, custom cabinetry, countertops, tile, lighting, appliance installation, paint and final styling. One contract, one crew, one project lead you can text.

How much do custom kitchen cabinets cost in Marin County?

Roughly $900 to $1,900 per linear foot depending on door style, finish and interior fittings. A typical family kitchen uses 24 to 38 linear feet. Building in our own Mill Valley shop removes the distributor margin.

What is the best countertop for a family kitchen?

Engineered quartz for most families — no etching, no sealing, survives everything. Quartzite if you want natural stone that still tolerates real life. Marble is best kept to a baking zone rather than the full run.

Can we really stay in the house the whole time?

Yes. Four hundred and thirty families have. The temporary kitchen goes in before demolition and stays until the new room genuinely works — and dust walls are sealed on day one, not after the mess starts.
