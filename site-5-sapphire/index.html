Why people put it off

The dentistry was never the hard part. The dread was.

Most people who need an implant, a crown or a deep cleaning already know they need it. What stops them is the arithmetic of it — a referral to a surgeon across town, a second opinion, a third appointment for the crown, and somewhere in the middle, an hour in a chair they have been avoiding since childhood.

So we removed the arithmetic. The surgeon, the restorative specialist and the anesthesiologist all work here. You are diagnosed once, planned once, and sedated by someone whose only job that day is you.

How it goes

Four visits, not four practices

The plan is written once, by the people who will carry it out.

  1. 01

    Diagnose properly

    A full exam with digital X-rays and, where implants or airway are involved, a 3D CBCT scan. We look at gums, bone, bite and airway — not just the tooth that hurts.

  2. 02

    One written plan

    Both specialists review your case together and you leave with a sequenced plan and one written figure. Financing and membership savings are applied before you decide.

  3. 03

    Treatment, your way

    Awake, lightly relaxed with nitrous, or fully sedated with Dr. Walbom monitoring you throughout. Weighted blanket, headphones and your own screen are standard, not extras.

  4. 04

    Keep it that way

    Maintenance is where the investment is protected. Membership members get their cleanings, screenings and X-rays built in, on a schedule set by what your gums actually need.

Questions people actually ask

Before you call

What makes Sapphire Dental Studio different from a general dental office?

Three board-certified specialists practise in one building: a periodontist who places implants, a prosthodontist who restores them, and a dentist anesthesiologist who handles sedation. In most towns those are three separate referrals, three consultations and three treatment plans. Here the plan is written once, by the people who will carry it out.

Can I be asleep for my dental treatment?

Yes. Dr. Michael Walbom is a dentist anesthesiologist, so sedation from nitrous oxide through to full IV sedation is delivered and monitored by a dedicated specialist whose only responsibility during your appointment is your comfort and safety — not by the dentist performing the treatment.

How much do dental implants cost in Winter Garden?

Cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced and whether bone grafting is needed, so an honest number requires a CBCT scan and an exam. After that you receive one written figure. CareCredit and Cherry financing spread it monthly, and membership plan members receive 15% off.

Do you take dental insurance?

We file with most PPO plans as a courtesy. If you have no insurance, the in-house membership plans start at $550 a year and include cleanings, exams, all diagnostic X-rays, oral cancer and sleep apnea screening, plus 15% off everything else — with no deductibles, no waiting periods and no claims to submit.

What is a fixed hybrid denture?

It is a full arch of teeth anchored to four to six dental implants and fixed in place, so it is never removed for cleaning and does not move when you eat or speak. Dr. Dugas places the implants and Dr. Chung designs and fits the prosthesis — both in this building.

Do you treat sleep apnea?

Yes. We screen every patient for airway and sleep concerns, and treat diagnosed obstructive sleep apnea with custom oral appliance therapy — an alternative for people who cannot tolerate CPAP. Dr. Dugas is pursuing board certification in dental sleep medicine.
