Choosing

Which one is yours

Choose Classic
Your gums are healthy and you need prevention: two cleanings, exams, X-rays and screenings. This is the standard adult plan and the one most members hold.
Choose Restore
You have a history of gum disease. Periodontal maintenance is three visits a year rather than two, because the interval is what controls the disease — and the deep cleaning itself is 35% off.

Financing

Can't pay all at once? Nobody expects you to.

Implants, full-arch work and full-mouth rehabilitation are real money. We work with CareCredit and Cherry, so treatment is spread across monthly payments at a rate you agree to before anything starts.

Both are applied for in the chair and decided in minutes. Cherry runs a soft credit check that does not affect your score to show you what you qualify for. You will know your monthly figure before you say yes to the treatment plan — not after.

Questions

About membership

Is a dental membership plan the same as insurance?

No. It is a direct agreement between you and the practice, so there is no third party deciding what is covered. That is why there are no deductibles, no annual maximum, no waiting periods, no claims to submit and no rejections.

What if I need treatment beyond what the plan includes?

Every member receives 15% off all other treatment, and the Restore plan adds 35% off deep cleaning while Renew adds 20% off new dentures and repairs. Larger treatment can also be financed through CareCredit or Cherry.

Which membership plan should I choose?

Classic suits healthy gums needing routine prevention. Restore suits anyone with a history of gum disease, because it provides three periodontal maintenance visits rather than two standard cleanings. Renew is built for denture wearers. If you are unsure, the exam will tell you which one fits.

Do you accept dental insurance as well?

Yes, we file with most PPO plans as a courtesy. The membership plans exist for people who have no insurance, or whose plan has an annual maximum so low that it runs out before the treatment is finished.
