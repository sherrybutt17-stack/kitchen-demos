01 / Vaquero, Westlake

A dark approach and one useless camera.

The existing system had a single camera over the front door, aimed at the porch ceiling. Everything that mattered — the motor court, the side return, the gate — was unlit and unwatched. Eleven cameras, two access-controlled doors, one working day.

11 cameras · 2 access doors · monitored alarm takeover · Alarm.com

02 / Alliance corridor

Thirty-four cameras and an insurer to satisfy.

A distribution tenant whose policy required 60 days of retention on every dock and the full perimeter of the lot. The previous installer had delivered 9 days. We re-sized the storage, re-aimed eleven cameras and added plate-capable angles at both gates.

34 cameras · 60-day retention · 2 plate-capture gates · multi-site dashboard
