Questions

On the money, specifically.

The four that decide it for most people.

Is there really no contract?

No contract of any length. Monitoring bills monthly and you cancel with thirty days' notice. The equipment is yours outright from day one because you bought it rather than leasing it, so leaving does not mean surrendering hardware or paying off a balance.

What does a security system cost to install in DFW?

Most homes land between $1,800 and $4,600 installed, driven by camera count, cable difficulty and whether access control is included. Commercial sites start around $6,500. You get one fixed written figure after the coverage survey, and it is valid for 60 days.

Can I finance the equipment?

Yes, over 12 to 60 months through our lending partner, with a soft credit check that does not affect your score to see your rate. A $3,200 system over 36 months is roughly $99 a month. Financing covers equipment and installation, never the monitoring fee.

Do camera-only systems have a monthly fee?

None at all. Cameras record to an NVR you own and you view them on your phone at no charge. A monthly fee only starts if you want a central station watching an alarm and dispatching police, which is a separate and entirely optional service.
