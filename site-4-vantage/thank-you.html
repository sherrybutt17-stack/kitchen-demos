No mystery, no drip campaign, no "a specialist will reach out." Three steps, and the first one is today.

01

Today

A technician — not a call centre — rings you to agree a time. If you asked for dusk, we book dusk. If nobody picks up we text rather than calling four more times.

02

At the survey

90 minutes to two hours walking the property together. You get the drawn coverage plan and a fixed written price before we leave the driveway.

03

Then nothing

The price holds for 60 days. We will follow up once. If the answer is no, it is genuinely no, and the plan is still yours to use however you like.

04

If you say yes

Most homes are installed within ten working days of accepting. One day on site, and we do not leave until you can run the system yourself.

While you wait

Two things worth doing before we arrive.

  • Walk it once at night. Stand at the street and at each gate and note which way you'd come in if you didn't want to be seen. That list is the survey's starting point.
  • Find your existing paperwork. If you have an alarm panel already, the model number and who monitors it tells us in thirty seconds whether a takeover saves you money.
